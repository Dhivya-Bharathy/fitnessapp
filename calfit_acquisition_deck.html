02 — The problem

Most fitness apps are trackers, not coaches.

The apps people use today are either feature-thin or expensive — and none of them do AI-first coaching at the depth users actually need.

Generic workouts. Most apps give static plans that don't adapt to equipment, fitness level, or available time.
Food logging is a chore. Manually searching databases is slow and inaccurate. Users abandon after a few days.
No real coaching. Chat-based AI coaches in existing apps are surface-level or locked behind expensive subscriptions.
No local context. Global apps ignore regional food databases — a Nigerian user logging "Jollof Rice" gets no results, or wrong macros.
Solo tracking doesn't stick. Fitness is behavioral. Without accountability, most users quit within 2 weeks.
03 — The solution

An AI-first fitness app that does the thinking for the user.

CalFit replaces static plans with live AI generation — workouts, meal plans, food recognition, and conversational coaching all generated on demand, personalized to the user's goals, equipment, and dietary context.

Generate, don't prescribe

AI builds a custom workout or meal plan in seconds based on what the user has, wants, and can do.

Scan food with the camera

Point the phone at a meal. Vision AI identifies the food and estimates macros — no typing needed.

Built-in accountability

Partner system with shared streaks, side-by-side dashboards, and real-time chat keeps users consistent.

No sign-up required — anonymous auth gets users into the app in under 30 seconds.
Localized for Nigerian users with a 25-entry local food database and regionally-aware AI prompting.
Voice input for the AI coach via Deepgram STT. Voice output with TTS workout cues during sessions.

05 — Features

Feature depth across 10 categories.

AI Workout Gen

Level, goal, duration, equipment inputs. Warmup, exercises, cooldown. Save and regenerate.

AI Meal Plans

7-step questionnaire. Goal, budget, cuisine, dietary restrictions, calorie target. Nigerian food-aware.

AI Chat Coach

Persistent chat history. Voice input. Inline workout generation from chat commands.

Food Scanner

Camera or gallery. Vision AI (Llama 3.2 90B). Returns macros and logs directly to the meal.

Progress Analytics

Week/month/quarter/year views. Calorie, workout, sleep, step, weight charts. PDF export.

Accountability

Up to 3 partners. Shared streak dashboard. Real-time chat via Supabase Realtime.

Streaks & Gamification

Daily check-ins. Milestone badges at 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, 90, 180, 365 days. Push reminders.

Health Tracking

Sleep logs. Step pedometer. Body measurements. Intermittent fasting timer. Water intake.
